New flow idea for app:

So the new flow of the idea for the app is that for every motion we first have a bank of motions to select from, and maybe later a way to design motions based on an LLM input where you can put in a topic or concept idea, but that would be secondary and not initially. From then on, from the bank and examples we have of motions, users can select one and go through the entire flow. Reading a motion will not be segregated from the CLI framework; it will be built in together. For each motion, they get not only the practice of reading the motion but also see how it ties into the claims, the link, and the impact, and they also have a progress bar on the side. After they have gone through the entire process and seen how it all connects for a single example of a motion, they can optionally have activities on the side later on either on the home page or somewhere else that enable them to work on specific parts of the flow individually. For example, if they want to practice only reading motions, or only claims, only links, only impact, or maybe even debate prep, etc.
